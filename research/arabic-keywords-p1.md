## 1. Method and limits (read first)

- **Real searches, no volumes yet.** I collected Google autocomplete suggestions (what people actually type) for 59 Arabic seed phrases. I then searched the top phrases and read the three competitor pages that rank.
- **Country split did not work.** Google returned the same suggestions for Saudi Arabia, UAE and Kuwait. It follows the location of the computer that asks (Egypt) more than the country setting. So the suggestions show general Arabic demand. Gulf signals are still visible, such as Saudi app names and Riyadh, Dubai and Kuwait in queries.
- **To finish P1 we need search volumes per country.** The free way: Google Ads Keyword Planner (needs a Google Ads account, no spend). Paste the 25 phrases in section 4, set locations Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, language Arabic, and export. Ahrefs or Similarweb also work, but their plugins here need sign-in.

## 2. What Arabic searchers type

1. **"برمجة" is as common as "تطوير".** People search both "شركة برمجة تطبيقات" (app programming company) and "شركة تطوير تطبيقات" (app development company). Use both: "تطوير" in headings, "برمجة" in body and metadata.
2. **Location is added to the query.** Suggestions include "في السعودية", "في الرياض", "في جدة", "في دبي", "في الكويت" and "أفضل شركة برمجة تطبيقات في الرياض". Founders want a company that works in their country.
3. **"Mobile" has two words.** "الجوال" (Gulf, formal) dominates the suggestions. "الموبايل" also appears, and "تطبيق موبايل" shows up in cost questions. Recommendation: "الجوال" in copy, both in metadata.
4. **Cost questions name Saudi apps.** "كم تكلفة تطبيق مثل حراج / هنقرستيشن / مرسول / طلبات" (how much does an app like Haraj, HungerStation, Mrsool cost). This is a clear Saudi buyer signal and a strong guide topic. The answers people find today are freelance job posts quoting $100 to $5,000, which is very weak content.
5. **MVP stays in Latin.** "تطوير mvp" and "ما هو mvp في البرمجة" are searched. "نسخة أولية" and "نموذج أولي للتطبيق" returned no suggestions. Recommendation: write "النسخة الأولى (MVP)", keeping the Latin MVP.
6. **AI app builders are a real audience.** "انشاء تطبيق بالذكاء الاصطناعي" (make an app with AI) is the top suggestion for "إنشاء تطبيق". "lovable شرح", "lovable اشتراك" and "lovable ai شرح" are searched in Arabic. This is the Launch-Ready Review audience (V1.2) and the AI-built-app guides.
7. **People hire individuals.** "مبرمج تطبيقات سعودي", "مبرمج تطبيقات الكويت" and "مبرمج فري لانسر" are searched. This is the Scale angle: a full team (developers, testers, a project manager) instead of one freelancer.
8. **Idea stage is searched.** "تحويل فكرة الى تطبيق" (turn an idea into an app), "فكرة تطبيق ناجحة" and "دراسة جدوى تطبيق" are searched. Most "دراسة جدوى" (feasibility study) searches want a free PDF, so treat them as guide topics, not page targets.
9. **Arabic AI is searched.** "شات بوت عربي", "شات بوت عربي واتساب" and "وكيل ذكاء اصطناعي واتساب". This matches a real fact in 09: AI agents and chatbots in Arabic and English.
10. **Noise to avoid:** "منصة تطوير ..." is mostly government platforms, "مراجعة كود" is mostly mobile wallet codes, and "صيانة تطبيق" is mostly bank apps. Do not target these.

The first results for "شركة برمجة تطبيقات في السعودية", "شركة تطوير تطبيقات في دبي" and "تطوير MVP" are mostly international agencies with Arabic subsites, plus directory lists and job ads.

| Appinventiv, `appinventiv.com/ar/mobile-app-development-company-dubai/` | H1 "شركة تطوير تطبيقات الجوال في دبي، الإمارات"; big brand logos; awards | Fluent but generic; inconsistent numbers on the same page |

**The opening:** the Arabic results are weak. The competitors use translated copy, inflated numbers, exclamation-mark CTAs and headings that only repeat the city and keyword. Native, specific Arabic with honest proof, such as named Saudi and Kuwaiti reviews, fixed price on Build and a senior sign-off, can stand out. This confirms the `05` note that Arabic results "look softer".

## 5. Rules this gives the Arabic copy

- **Home title (DRAFT, ≤ 60 characters):** شركة برمجة تطبيقات للشركات الناشئة | DiscoverDev
- **Home meta (DRAFT):** نبني تطبيقات الجوال والويب للشركات الناشئة في السعودية والكويت: نختبر الفكرة، ونبني النسخة الأولى (MVP) خلال 8 إلى 12 أسبوعًا، والكود ملكك.
- **H1 stays the brand promise, not a keyword string.** The keyword goes in the title, meta, label and first paragraph. Competitors' "company in city" H1s read as spam.
- **Countries:** name Saudi Arabia and Kuwait (real clients in 09). Do not claim UAE clients. Gulf hours apply to all three.
- **MVP:** "النسخة الأولى (MVP)" on first use, then "النسخة الأولى".
- **Clarity call name (two options for the reviewer):** "مكالمة توضيحية مجانية" (closest) or "مكالمة استكشافية مجانية" (more natural for some Gulf readers). Keep one everywhere.
