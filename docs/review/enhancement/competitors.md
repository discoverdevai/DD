## Gaps and opportunities
1. **High: the method name clashes with TrianglZ's "AI-Native Agile v2.0".** They define it in their llms.txt, so AI engines may mix the two up. Mohamed needs to decide: rename it, or keep it with a clearly different descriptor. Log it in `11` before launch.
2. **High: there is no "how we work" explanation of who does what.** Add a table per stage showing what AI does and what a senior engineer signs off (home #agile-v2 and /about).
3. **High: Insights only serves the secondary buyer.** Add guides for founders building new products: MVP timeline, how fixed-price MVPs work, when to bring in an outside team, what to cut.
4. **High: nobody owns "MVP development Egypt / MENA / Gulf"**, in English or Arabic. Add a regional FAQ on /build, region wording on /about and in llms.txt, and prioritise Arabic routes.
5. **High: the Review shows no price**, while every rescue specialist does. Resolve `[PRICE]` with a "from" figure; keep it hidden until then.
6. **Medium: no qualifying step on Review booking.** Add a "Where are you?" picker (launching soon / already live / developer left).
7. **Medium: thin proof.** Link the Clutch profile and use consented quotes from `09`.
8. **Medium: the AI-cost case is used only once.** Write the C9 cost article and link it from home.
9. **Medium: we don't explain how we take over an existing codebase.** Cover it on /scale and in the takeover FAQ.
10. **Low: llms.txt** needs the case link, region wording and a "not to be confused with" line.
11. **Low: we don't say who takes the call.** Gated by `[TEAM]`.
