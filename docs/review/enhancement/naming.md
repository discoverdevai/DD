## 2025–2026 trends (for direction only; mostly vendor sources)
- **T1 Accountability.** Trust in AI code is low and checking is inconsistent. Sources: [Sonar 2026](https://www.sonarsource.com/blog/state-of-code-developer-survey-report-the-current-reality-of-ai-coding/); [GitLab, June 2026](https://about.gitlab.com/press/releases/2026-06-23-gitlab-research-reveals-organizations-are-generating-ai-code-faster-than-they-can-control-it/), which asks who is responsible for code once it's live; senior sign-off rules at large companies (second-hand report).
- **T2 Review beats speed.** Tools now sell speed (Cursor, Replit, Lovable). The human role is described as steering and reviewing (Claude Code: "Steer and review"; GitHub: "Command your craft").
- **T3 Ownership and maintainability.** IP, technical debt and the vibe-coding cleanup market.
- **T4 Continuity.** Buyers want partners who own outcomes and stay.
- **T5 Plain words.** A reaction against AI buzzwords.
