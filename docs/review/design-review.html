The verdict

The site is clean, fast and honest, and every page works. It reads as a well-made kit rather than as DiscoverDev. Three things hold every score at 6.5 or lower: no idea that only we own, no real pictures, and template devices repeated on every page.

Eight fixes that lift every page

Ranked by how much they change the whole site. The first is already done.

1

Brand fonts on first visit Fixed today

Rubik and JetBrains Mono showed as Arial and Courier for new visitors. They now load properly (commit c66b19d).

2

White-led colour, as the brand guide says

About 65% of every page is near-black navy; the guide asks for white on about 60%. Open in navy, then turn white. Keep navy for openers and the closing band only.

3

One picture of how we work

Rename "Agile v2" to The DiscoverDev Way and draw it once: the keystone arch. Use that one system to replace the card grids (six check areas, five partner reasons, five cost changes).

4

Headlines in two lines

Nine pages run three or four lines. Keep the product name as the headline and move the rest into the lead, through 03-copy first.

5

Mono means AI, and only AI

Monospace now covers about ten jobs (numbers, tags, countries, labels), so it signals nothing. Keep it inside product fragments where AI wrote the line.

6

Real pictures, honestly sourced

Zero today. Start with real interface fragments (a findings report, a pull request with a senior's approval), then team and hub photos, then credited Alexandria archive images.

7

Fewer outlines, glows and numbers

Hairline boxes on 13 of 13 pages, glows on 10, 01/02 numbering on 8. Use tone steps instead of lines, and keep numbers where order is real.

8

Close the holes

Empty hero halves (Insights, About, Launch check), one-line sections (Work rating, Partners process), 3+2 orphan grids, and the "Alexandri a" word break.

Three art directions

Each one turns our own story into the picture. TrianglZ owns cooking and taste; we own construction and accountability: a named role, a signature, a gate.

Recommended system

A. The keystone arch

The arrow mark's two shards become the stones of an arch. Many small blue stones are laid by AI, and one navy keystone is set last by a senior.

Live thing: as you scroll The DiscoverDev Way, each step adds a course of stones. The arch only locks at "Seniors sign off".

Draws on The New Industrials, Pentagram MIT Media Lab, Stripe's wave roles, The Queue.

For About and the Alexandria story

B. The drawing set

The project's drawing set becomes the layout: rough marker sketches for Discover, precise drawings for Build, and the senior's sign-off as the stamp in the title block.

Live thing: drag a slider to turn a rough discovery sketch into the finished drawing of the same screen.

Draws on Illoca, Basecamp Shape Up, Mosby's Files, Oxide.
