# Home V1.0: CRO, marketing and copy review

Reviewed 2026-10-18 against `dist/index.html`, the screenshots in this folder, `autocheck.json`, `03` §2V1/§2A, `09`, `11` §0b, `02`, and the site-map plan §8–9. Read-only: no site files were changed.
**Rule for every fix below:** copy changes go into `context/03-copy.md` first, then the build. Facts come from `09` only.

## Verdict in one paragraph
The page does its main job. In 5 seconds a visitor gets what you do (build a live product), how long it takes (8–12 weeks), how (senior engineers plus AI), the ownership promise, and one clear free action, with the rating right under the button. Every one of the 10 CTAs points to `/book?type=clarity`, which fits a one-goal page. The real risks are in the proof. A few items break decisions already recorded in `11` or `09`: Rushr, the clinic, NJWAR's country and AI claim, Ireland on the map, and EduLens named next to the metric from its anonymized case. Second, the "problem" section names no problem, and the method is said 5–6 times while the visitor's own struggle is never said once.
