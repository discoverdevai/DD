# Home V1.0: frontend, UI/UX and brand review

Date: 2026-10-18 · Page: `dist/index.html` at http://127.0.0.1:4321/ · Read-only review, no site code changed.
Method: Playwright at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1366, 1440 and 1920 (layout boxes, overflow, tap targets, anchor jumps, 45-step Tab walk, CLS/LCP observers, running animations with and without `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`), plus a visual pass over `docs/review/home-v1/*.png` and new shots in `docs/review/home-v1/fe/`.
Checked against `context/06-design-system.md`, `context/01-brand.md` and `src/styles/tokens.css`.

## What is already good (keep it)
- **No horizontal scroll at any width.** `scrollWidth` equals the viewport at all 7 widths. The overflow rows in autocheck are clipped decoration: hero drift `<i>`, the marquee track, and chapter devices inside `.ch { overflow: clip }`.
- **Performance looks healthy.** Local LCP is 456 ms (390) and 480 ms (1440), and the LCP element is the H1 text. CLS is 0 on mobile and 0.009 on desktop after a full scroll. Page JS is about 1 KB plus 3.4 KB analytics. NJWAR images are 12 to 42 KB WebP with `srcset`. HTML is 208 KB raw and 54 KB gzipped.
- **Reduced motion works.** `document.getAnimations()` returns 0 running animations under `reduce`, against 21 normally.
- **Focus is visible on every stop.** It is a 2px white outline on ink, and cards show it on the card through `.card-link:has(a:focus-visible)`. The skip link works. Tab order follows the visual order.
- **The hero is strong.** The two-layer sprint board (mono "AI drafts" and green "senior approved") is distinctive and on-brief. It reflows well from 768 (`fe/768-hero.png`).
- **No false positive on the empty element.** The 0-height item autocheck flagged in `<main>` is the ProofBand `<script>` (the 3rd child of `main`), not an empty layout box. It still causes a side effect, listed under P2.

## P0: broken
None found. Nothing is visually broken, overflowing or keyboard-trapped at any tested width.
