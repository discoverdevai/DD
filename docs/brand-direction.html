The brand idea

// internal brand idea, not site copy

machine speed,
human sign-off.

Every AI studio now says it is fast. Every agency says it is careful. DiscoverDev is the one that can show both on the same screen: AI does the heavy lifting, and a senior engineer signs off every line that reaches users. So every visual on the site has two layers, and you can always tell which is which.

Two voices, one system

AI  check data rules on every table
AI  trace payment events end to end

The machine voice: JetBrains Mono, signal blue, small, dense, fast. Logs, progress, file names.

Verdict: fix, don't rebuild
Signed off by a senior engineer

The human voice: Manrope, big, calm, with a mint check. Decisions, verdicts, sign-offs.

Brand codes

  • The run panel. AI steps stream, then human sign-offs land. The site's signature, replacing a hero illustration.
  • The blade beam. One light shape cut from the logo's own chevron angle. The only glow on the page, and it is ours, not a stock streak.
  • Hairline grid. A precise 80px grid that fades out. Engineering order, very quiet.
  • Layer chips. AI-accelerated and Senior sign-off tags on process steps, so the method is visible everywhere.
  • Mint means done by a person. Mint (#7EF0C0, from your brand profile) only ever marks human sign-off or "in production".

Palette and type

ink navy brand blue signal sign-off paper

All built from your brand blues; no purple, no rainbow gradients. Manrope 800 display with tight tracking, Rubik body, JetBrains Mono as the machine voice (already used in your company profile deck).

Keep, and leave out

  • Keep the copy rules: plain words, no "AI-native", no hype. The visuals say "technical"; the words say "we'll tell you plainly".
  • Keep proof next to claims: the run panel is an example, labelled as one.
  • Leave out robots, brains, sparkle icons, purple gradients, glass on everything, logo marquees and stock photos.
Research behind this: category leaders (Linear, Vercel, Resend, Cursor, Factory) share a precise grid, a mono "machine voice", real product artifacts and one bold device. Sources: Veza Digital, AI web trends 2026 · DesignRush, "polished is the new generic" · IRPR, dark mode 2026

Signal

Dark, premium, engineered. An ink-blue canvas, a fading hairline grid and one light beam cut from the logo's chevron. The hero is the run panel: AI steps stream in, then a senior engineer and a product lead sign off. It is the most "AI company" of the three while staying calm.

Lab

The look of today's most respected AI tool makers: a cool light-grey lab, black ink type, crosshair marks and mono labels. The chevron is drawn as a dot-matrix (the machine texture), and a white sign-off card sits on top of it (the human layer). Quieter and rarer in your market than dark.

My recommendation: D, Signal

  • It is the clearest answer to "techie, AI, premium", and it turns your real method into the hero instead of decorating around it.
  • It evolves the dark navy people already know, so it reads as DiscoverDev, grown up.
  • Articles, legal pages and the booking calendar would use a light "paper" surface with the same type and mono voice, so long reading stays comfortable and Cal.com fits in.
