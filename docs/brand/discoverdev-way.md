# The DiscoverDev Way

*Brand and strategy on one page. Proposed Oct 16, 2026. Replaces "Agile v2". Facts come from `context/09-proof.md`.*

---

## 1. In one line
**Discover first. Build with AI. Seniors sign off.**

## 2. What we believe
> AI made software fast.
> Fast is easy to buy now.
> What's rare is a senior who stands behind every release.
> That's the DiscoverDev Way.

## 3. The way, in three moves
| Move | What we do | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover first** | We check it's the right thing before any code | No money spent building the wrong product |
| **Build with AI** | AI does the heavy lifting, with results every week | Live in 8–12 weeks, at a fixed price |
| **Seniors sign off** | A senior engineer signs off every release | Someone answers for every line your users see |

## 4. The picture
**AI lays the bricks. A senior sets the keystone.**
The keystone is the last stone set, and the whole arch depends on it. That's our job on every release.

## 5. Where we stand
- **For:** founders and product teams who need the right product, built fast.
- **Against:** speed with nobody accountable.
- **Different from:**
  - AI tools, which sell speed.
  - TrianglZ, which sells taste.
  - We sell a senior's sign-off.
- **Always:** full IP, no lock-in, and one team that stays.

## 6. The promise
**Fast, and someone answers for it.**

## 7. Proof (only from `09`)
- 5.0 across 11 reviews
- 8–12 week builds, against a typical 12–16
- 60–90% lower AI cost (the EdTech case)
- A senior engineer signs off every release

## 8. Say it / never say it
| Say | Never |
|---|---|
| Short sentences, plain words | Hype, exclamation marks, em dashes in short copy |
| Who does what: "AI drafts, a senior signs off" | cheap, offshore, cutting-edge, world-class, leverage |
| "The DiscoverDev Way" | "Agile v2", "DiscoverDev Method", "Discover Dev" |
| What we can prove | Anything `09` doesn't back |

## 9. Show it
- **Keystone mark:** a small icon on every "signed off" step.
- **Green check:** the human sign-off.
- **Colour:** navy for openers, blue to point, white for reading.

## 10. One rule for search and AI answers
Use the same name and the same definition everywhere: the site, `llms.txt`, schema, Clutch and LinkedIn.
> *The DiscoverDev Way: we find the right thing to build first, AI does the heavy lifting, and a senior engineer signs off every release.*

## 11. Where it lives on the site
- **Home:**
  - Pill: "The DiscoverDev Way · senior-signed".
  - The method section: the line, the picture and the three moves.
  - The "How we work" nav link.
- **Discovery files:** the meta description, `llms.txt` and Organization schema.
- **Other pages:** the Build stage, the service statements, and About's "How we work".

## 12. How we'll know it works
- People search for "DiscoverDev Way".
- AI answers quote our definition.
- Visitors click the clarity call from the method section.
