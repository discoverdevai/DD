Review on the desk

Your design system (06) exactly as written. A bright white page, navy headings, blue only on actions. The hero carries the findings panel: what a Launch-Ready Review actually produces. Calm, specific, evidence first.

Strengths

  • Every contrast pair already checked in 06 §3

Watch out

Night shift

Your Figma file (DiscoverDev Landing Page DD) as drawn, with the new copy poured in. Dark navy hero with cyan light streaks, a grid texture, frosted glass buttons, centred type, window-style cards with code file names, // .config pills on every section.

Strengths

  • Continuity with the current site and the Figma
  • Striking first screen, reads as "tech company" instantly

Watch out

  • Looks like most AI and dev-shop sites, including Robusta's "AI-native" hero you want to stand apart from
  • Text over streaks and glass is hard to keep at 4.5:1 (the old repo measured 1.24:1 over the streak)
  • Glow, blur and big hero art cost speed on phones
  • The findings panel has no natural home, so the offer reads as generic

Rules it changes

  • 06 §1 "Avoid": glows, glassmorphism, heavy terminal look, motif on every section
  • 06 §5: navy on more than 2 sections
  • 06 §9.11: hero surface

CRecommended

Navy desk

The first impression your visitors know (solid brand navy, a faint grid from the Figma hero), with the substance of A. The findings panel sits on the navy like a report on a dark desk and overlaps into the white page below. Everything after the hero is A: light, calm, proof next to claims. The Figma's window chrome and // .config pill are kept as accents, twice per page at most.

Strengths

  • Recognisably DiscoverDev from the first second
  • The Review artifact is the hero's focal point, with stronger contrast than in A
  • White text on #032993 is 11.95:1; no glow or glass to fight
  • Same speed as A (solid colour, no hero image)

Watch out

  • Hero and closing band are both navy, so the middle of the page must stay light
  • The overlap needs care at tablet widths (handled with container queries)

Rules it changes

  • 06 §9.11: hero surface becomes navy (was white)
  • 06 §5 home map: the offer spotlight moves to a light surface so navy stays at 2 sections

My recommendation: C, Navy desk

  • It keeps the navy first impression people already link to DiscoverDev, so the new site feels like a step up, not a different company.
  • It puts the Launch-Ready Review findings at the centre of the first screen, which is what the whole strategy rests on.
  • It stays inside your accessibility and speed budgets without special cases.
  • From B it keeps the navy, the grid texture, the window chrome and the // .config pill. It drops the glow streaks, glass, robot imagery and logo wall, which your own design system rules out.
