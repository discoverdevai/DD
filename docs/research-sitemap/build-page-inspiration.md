## 2. Ideas per DiscoverDev section

**1. Hero + "How a Build runs" panel**
- Turn the panel into a 3-row mini track (Validate 1–2w, Build 8–12w, Launch) with the week span drawn as a bar. Source: Halo Lab week ranges, https://halo-lab.com/services/mvp-development
- Add a two-line "Best for / Not for" under the subhead. Source: Wavect, https://wavect.io/services/vibe-coding-rescue/
- Keep one primary CTA in the hero; Red Leg has none in its hero and loses the reader there. Source: https://redleg.dev/vibe-coding

**2. Start where you are (two path cards)**
- Phrase each card as a situation ("I have an idea" / "I already built it with AI"), as Designli's founder-stage path does: https://www.designli.co/
- Put a small "then" arrow on each card into Build so both paths visibly join. Source: Empat's AI-driven discovery leading into the MVP on its homepage, https://www.empat.tech/
- Make the Review card's headline thoughtbot's question style ("Outgrown your prototype?"): https://thoughtbot.com/services/mvp-development

**3. Validate decision board**
- Number the 4 steps 01–04 in the Linear Method style, with each outcome (Build / Change direction / Stop) as a labelled exit: https://linear.app/method
- State the fee credit beside the "Build" exit, the way MetaCTO marks steps Free / Paid: https://www.metacto.com/solutions/vibe-rescue
- Add a one-line "what you hold at the end" artefact list (prototype link, scope doc, fixed estimate). Source: Wavect's sample outputs.

**4. 8–12 week track**
- Add a "You are here" or "This week" marker plus a per-week demo dot. Source: Halo Lab's process marker.
- Label each phase with its deliverable, not just its name. Netguru's steps have no output and read as generic: https://www.netguru.com/services/mvp-development
- Add a small row marking where AI drafts and where the senior engineer signs off. No competitor shows this; Empat only lists tools.

**5. Review section + sample report**
- Open with a before/after code card (secret in code, missing auth check, fixed) next to the report. Sources: Red Leg's diff hero and MetaCTO's `app.ts` card.
- Offer the sample report as a download/preview, as Wavect does with its downloadable findings.
- Show price and days as two big facts ($1,490 / ~5 working days), which beats Red Leg's $1,500 over 2 weeks. Use the "Free call" wording carefully: Red Leg's "Free Code Audit" button contradicts its paid audit.

**6. What you get**
- Split it into "Outcomes" and an "Included" checklist, as Ramotion's What you get / Deliverables pairing does: https://www.ramotion.com/product/delivery/
- Add a short "Not included" list for trust. Source: MetaCTO's fit check and Wavect's "Not best for".

**7. Price, team and ownership**
- Lay it out as a document/SOW panel with rows (Price model, Team make-up, Who owns code, Change requests). Sources: Ramotion's tiers showing team composition, Empat's change-request process.
- If D-01 numbers are approved, add a "from" figure per engagement. Ramotion and Stormotion publish one. **Needs facts: `[PRICE]`, do not invent.**
- Gulf angle: add a short "Built for Gulf launches" line (Arabic/RTL, local payments). Source: Sendan, https://www.sendantech.com/en-SA/mobile-app-development-cost-saudi-arabia. **Only claim integrations DiscoverDev has shipped (needs a 09-proof check).**

**8. Proof (EduLens)**
- Use Netguru's case card: one image plus a 3-metric row. Only one EduLens metric (the 60–90% AI-cost range) is sourced, so use one big number plus 2 qualitative facts. **No invented metrics.**
- Pair the quote with name, role and company, as Railsware and thoughtbot do.
- Skip stat bars (years, projects, NPS) unless 09-proof has them. Monterail and Netguru lean on these. **Needs facts.**

**9. FAQ**
- Put the questions buyers ask first: cost, what if scope changes, who owns the code, rebuild vs. fix. Sources: Wavect's rebuild-vs-rescue callout and the FAQs on Halo Lab and Stormotion.
- Keep the answers consistent with the page. Netguru and Sendan contradict their own timelines, so 8–12 weeks must match everywhere.

**10. Closing CTA band**
- End on a question, like thoughtbot's "What does success look like for your product?", with a single button.
- Offer two doors that mirror section 2 (Book a Validate call / Send us your app for Review), as MetaCTO's "Send us the repo" does.
- Add a WhatsApp option for Gulf buyers (Sendan does). **Needs a 07 CSP/tech check and an approved number.**

## 3. Remove or merge to tighten the page
1. **Merge "Start where you are" into the hero** as two secondary links under the main CTA. Designli and MetaCTO route by situation right away, and a separate section repeats the hero.
2. **Merge "What you get" into "Price, team and ownership"** as one deliverables-and-terms panel. Ramotion keeps deliverables beside the investment, and two list-heavy sections in a row read as padding.
3. **Fold the Validate decision board into the 8–12 week track** as its first segment, with the exits drawn as branches. Halo Lab shows research and prototype as the opening steps of one track; two separate timeline visuals compete.
