## Bottom line
DiscoverDev is sold as **a product and engineering company that builds with Agile v2**: AI does the heavy lifting and senior engineers own the quality. We sell **the whole lifecycle** (Validate → Build → Scale) and a catalog of services. The Launch-Ready Review stays, but as **one way in** for people who already built something. It is no longer the front door. The site-wide primary action becomes **Book a free clarity call**.

- **Don't promise speed numbers we can't prove.** Only "8–12 weeks" (Build) and "1–2 weeks" (Validate) and "results every week", all in `09`. No "x times faster".
- **Review page and quiz keep working.** Their copy, schema and CTAs stay; only the home, nav, sticky bar and footer order change.
- **SEO.** Home title stays (already "Product & Engineering Partner for Founders"); meta changes to the new promise. Pillar content on AI-built apps still feeds /launch-ready.
