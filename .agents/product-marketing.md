# Product Marketing Context

Document version: 1.0 · Last updated: 2026-10-15
Read by the `mkt-*` skills. Detail lives in `context/01`, `02`, `08` and `09`, and in `research/market-voc.md`.

## 1. Product Overview
- **One line:** DiscoverDev is a product and engineering company that helps founders build the right product, ship it fast, and own all of it.
- **What it does:**
  - **Launch-Ready Review:** gets AI-built and freelancer-built MVPs ready for real users. Takes ~5 days, has a fixed fee and a guarantee.
  - **Validate, Build, Scale:** the follow-on work, done by senior, AI-accelerated teams.
- **Category:** boutique product and engineering studio, offering "AI-built app launch review and build".
- **Business model:** fixed-fee Review → fixed-price fix or Build → monthly Scale retainer. Partners pay for white-label delivery or staffing.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary:** launch-stuck founders in the US and GCC (KSA, UAE, KW), weighted equally.
  - Pre-seed, angel or self-funded.
  - Have a prototype built with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor or a freelancer, and can't launch it safely.
- **Channel:** software houses and agencies (white-label, staffing).
- **Referral-led:** idea-stage founders (Validate) and funded startups (Scale).
- **Main use case:** find what will break before real users and money arrive, then fix it or rebuild with a fixed price and date.

## 3. Personas
- **Solo or non-technical founder.** Decision maker and payer. Cares about launch date, data safety and spend.
- **Technical co-founder or freelancer.** Influencer. Cares about a fair verdict, keeping the code, and not being blamed.
- **Investor or partner.** Indirect influencer. Wants "who built this and is it secure?" answered.
- **Software-house owner or CTO** (partners). Cares about quality under their brand, NDA, IP and reliability.

## 4. Problems & Pain Points
- **It works in the demo, but isn't ready for strangers:**
  - Supabase RLS off, exposed keys, broken auth.
  - Payments fail.
  - Fixing one bug creates three more.
  - Credits burn.
  - The freelancer is gone.
- **Current options fall short:**
  - Free scanners give no verdict.
  - $490 gig audits are shallow.
  - US rescues cost $25K–150K.
  - Enterprise studios have no founder door.
- **Cost of doing nothing:** a data leak, a lost month, a surprise rewrite, lost investor trust.

## 5. Competitive Landscape
- **Direct:**
  - AI-app rescue specialists: RapidDev, Valletta, metacto, p0stman, Upwork gigs.
  - TrianglZ (same city).
- **Secondary:**
  - Boutique studios: Goji Labs, thoughtbot, Fueled.
  - Regional groups: Robusta Group, Enozom, RunProf.
- **Indirect:** keep prompting, free scanners, hiring a freelancer, rebuilding in-house.

## 6. Differentiation
- **Guarantee:** "No useful findings, no charge". Unique in this set.
- **Product lens:** a decision, not a report. Fix, refactor or rebuild, plus what to cut.
- **Senior people:** senior engineers own the quality, and the team stays after launch. Named people and photos only if D-16/D-17 approve.
- **Bilingual:** Arabic + English, with time zones overlapping the US and GCC.
- **AI cost proof:** EduLens AI costs down 60–90%.
- **Ownership:** your repo and your accounts from day one.

## 7. Objections & Anti-Personas
- **Objections:**
  - "A scanner is free." → Scanners list issues; we give a ranked verdict, a fix plan and a fixed quote.
  - "You'll push a rebuild." → The verdict is evidence-based, the fee is credited, and the guarantee applies.
  - "Offshore means juniors." → Senior team, weekly results; named people once D-16 allows.
- **Anti-personas:**
  - Enterprise RFPs.
  - Lowest-price shoppers.
  - Founders who want an idea built with no discovery.
  - Marketing-website-only jobs.

## 8. Switching Dynamics
- **Push:** leak stories, bug loops, credit burn, freelancer gone, investor diligence.
- **Pull:** fixed fee, ~5 days, plain verdict, fee credited, guarantee.
- **Habit:** keep prompting, ship anyway.
- **Anxiety:** upsell, rebuild fear, IP, offshore quality.

## 9. Customer Language
- "it works" and "it is ready for strangers" are completely different milestones
- "Always terrified about security but really don't even know where to begin"
- "tables where RLS was never enabled at all"
- "repeated fixes that did not fix the problem"
- "a simmering feeling of dread"
- **Use:** launch-ready, real users, verdict, fix or rebuild, you own it, fixed fee.
- **Avoid:** cheap, world-class, synergy, bare "AI-native".

## 10. Brand Voice
- **Tone:** confident, plain-spoken, founder-to-founder.
- **Style:** direct and specific. No hype, no exclamation marks, no em-dashes in short copy.
- **Personality:** partner, curious, product-minded, honest, calm.

## 11. Proof Points
- 5.0 across 11 reviews (Clutch, Upwork, Fiverr, direct).
- Clients on 4 continents.
- EduLens: AI costs down 60–90%.
- Rushr: US home-services marketplace built from zero in 16 weeks (metrics + consent pending, D-07).
- Retention: Athkado, Upscend, Arheb and Pyramakerz all expanded scope.
- All quotes and named clients need consent before use (see `context/09-proof.md` and D-05).

## 12. Goals
- **Business:** more inbound leads, bigger and recurring deals.
- **Key conversion:** book a Launch-Ready Review. Secondary: a free clarity call.
- **Metric:** calls and Reviews booked per month.
- **Q4 targets:** 9 calls, 3 Reviews, 12 inquiries.
