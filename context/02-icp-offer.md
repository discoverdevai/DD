## Core job
When my AI-built or freelancer-built MVP works in the demo but real users and real money are about to touch it, I want a senior team to tell me plainly what will break and what it costs to fix. Then I can launch without a data leak, a surprise rewrite or another lost month.

- **Functional:**
  - Find security holes (Supabase RLS, exposed keys, auth).
  - Make payments reliable.
  - Get a fix-or-rebuild call, a fixed price and a date.
- **Emotional:** stop the dread. Know what I don't know. Control the spend.
- **Social:**
  - Look credible to investors and early users.
  - GCC: work with a team that speaks my language.
- **Forces:**
  - **Push:** leak stories, bug loops, credit burn, freelancer gone, investor diligence.
  - **Pull:** fixed fee, ~5 days, plain verdict, fee credited, senior team, I own the code.
  - **Anxiety:**
    - "They'll tell me to rebuild everything."
    - Upsell.
    - Offshore juniors.
    - IP.
    - "A scanner is free."
  - **Habit:** keep prompting, a $490 gig audit, ship anyway.

## Positioning gaps we own (ranked)
1. The only Egypt/GCC studio speaking to founders with AI-built apps, bilingual.
2. The only rescue offer with a guarantee.
3. A decision, not just a report. Product people judge fix, refactor or rebuild, and what to cut.
4. Mid-band price honesty between $199 scans and $25K–150K rescues.
5. Show who does the work: senior people, not a hidden team.
6. AI cost proof, not AI claims: EduLens cut AI costs 60–90%.
7. Ownership by default: your repo and accounts from day one.

**Don't fight on:** logo walls, review volume, team size, office count.
