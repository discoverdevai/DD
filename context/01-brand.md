# 01 · Brand

## Positioning
For founders, funded startups and software houses in the US, GCC and Egypt who need to build the right product and grow it, DiscoverDev is the product and engineering partner that discovers before it builds, ships with senior AI-accelerated teams, and stays to scale.

- **Core idea (repeat everywhere):** "Most teams just build what you ask for. We help you build the right thing, then ship it fast, and you own all of it."
- **Tagline:** "Senior teams that ship faster, without cutting corners."
- **Pillars:** Discover first · Senior teams that stay · AI that pays for itself.
- **Known for:** product discovery and MVP · dedicated senior teams · AI in production.
- **Category:** boutique product and engineering studio, not an outsourcing body shop.

## Personality and voice
- Partner, founder-friendly, curious, product-minded. Premium value, not corporate.
- Confident, plain-spoken, founder-to-founder. Specific beats vague. No hype.
- No exclamation marks. No em-dashes in short copy.
- **Lead with:** quality + speed, product-first, full lifecycle ("we stay").
- **Quiet reassurance, never the headline:**
  - IP ownership and no lock-in.
  - Cost. It is a consequence of the model, never the hook.
  - "US presence". It reassures, it doesn't sell.
- **Accent motif:** `// .config` and `$` command lines. Flavour only, never the message.
- **Words to use:** launch-ready, real users, senior, you own it, fixed fee, verdict, fix or rebuild.
- **Words to avoid:**
  - cheap, affordable
  - world-class, cutting-edge, synergy
  - "AI-native" as a bare claim (use the EduLens number instead)
- **AI app builders** (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor): tools we respect and use, never mocked.

## Visual direction: Signal (approved 2026-10-15)
- **Brand idea (internal, not site copy):** machine speed, human sign-off. Every visual has two layers you can tell apart:
  - **AI layer:** JetBrains Mono, signal blue, small and dense (checks, logs, file names).
  - **Human layer:** Manrope, calm and large, a green check (decisions, verdicts, sign-offs).
- **Surfaces:** the workshop and the report. Dark ink (`#01082D`, derived from brand navy) for heroes and process; light paper for offers, FAQs, articles, legal and booking; navy for the closing band.
- **Signature:** the RunPanel (an example Review run) in the hero. The logo blade is the only graphic device. No glows, glass, robots or stock.
- Sources: `docs/creative-brief-signal.md`, `docs/design-system-signal.md`, `docs/design-critique-round2.md`. Spec: `06-design-system.md` §0.

## Identity (full spec in `06-design-system.md`)
- **Colours:**
  - Primary `#1240BE`
  - Dark navy `#032993` (dark sections, openers)
  - White `#FEFEFE` (content backgrounds)
  - Black `#0F0F0F` (body text)
  - Grey `#858585` (large text and decoration only). Small muted text uses `#6B6B6B`.
- **Dark canvas (Signal):** ink `#01082D` and raised `#02103E`, both on the brand blue hue. Never a generic near-black.
- **Mono (Signal):** JetBrains Mono for the AI layer, eyebrows and the `$` motif (from the company profile deck).
- **Fonts:**
  - Manrope for headlines.
  - Rubik for body, bullets and tables.
  - IBM Plex Sans Arabic for `/ar` (to confirm, D-12).
- **Logo:** blue arrow/chevron mark above the "DiscoverDev" wordmark, with "Dev" in blue.
  - Only a 500px JPG is in `assets/logo/`. SVGs are needed (D-33).
  - The full-colour logo fails contrast on navy, so a reversed version is needed.

## Brand architecture
- DiscoverDev is the master brand.
- **Offers:** Launch-Ready Review (front door), then Validate, Build, Scale. Partners (white-label and staffing) is a separate track.
- **Yawmy Care:** endorsed "by DiscoverDev". Product page comes in 2027; mention only as proof of product DNA.

## Markets and language
- **Markets:**
  - US and GCC (KSA, UAE, KW), weighted equally.
  - Egypt/MENA, secondary.
  - UK/EU: inbound only.
- **Language:** English first. Arabic-ready build; `/ar` in Q1 2027.
- **Hub:** Egypt (Alexandria) is the only engineering hub. Arabic + English, GMT+2.
