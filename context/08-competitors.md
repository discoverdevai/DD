## Robusta Group: what to know
- **Who they are:** enterprise and government transformation group from Cairo, with offices in Riyadh, Dubai, London and Berlin. Claims 250+ clients and $500M+ GMV, with logos like Vodafone, PepsiCo, Saudi Tourism Authority and Tawuniya.
- **Their site:**
  - Hero: "Building MEA's AI-Native Future".
  - CTA: "Get In Touch". Contact is email only, with no booking.
  - No prices.
  - No founder or MVP path. Never says "startup" or "MVP".
- **Their proof:** 0 Clutch reviews; 4.5 from 5 on Sortlist. Metric-led case studies, but no client quotes or baselines.
- **Their content:** 67 articles, none on MVPs or AI-built apps. English only.
- **Beat them on:**
  - A founder door: the Review.
  - Price signal.
  - Instant booking.
  - Client voice (Clutch 5.0).
  - Visible senior team.
  - AI cost proof.
  - Launch content.
- **Don't fight on:** logos, scale, offices.

## Patterns across the field
- Rescue specialists speak in second person ("Your app works. Is it actually secure?"). Repo-first CTAs are common.
- Read-only access and fee credited to the fix are now table stakes.
- **Nobody offers a guarantee.** Ours sits next to the CTA.
- Studios publish no prices. Rescue shops do.
- No Egypt or GCC firm has a vibe-code or AI-app rescue offer.
